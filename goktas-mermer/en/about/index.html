The Studio

We shape permanence from raw earth.

Marble, travertine and onyx — cut, bookmatched and finished into surfaces that outlive the spaces they define. Every commission begins at the block, reading the vein before a single cut is made.

Material First

The stone decides. We listen, then cut.

A vein is a record of pressure and time. Reading it well is the difference between a surface and a signature — which is why selection at the quarry is where every project truly begins.

What We Work In

Materials

Calacatta, Statuario, Nero Marquina and backlit onyx — sourced block by block, bookmatched and finished for feature walls, vanities and sculptural surfaces.

Travertine, limra and küfeki stone — breathing, ageing stones laid across floors and façades where permanence and patina matter more than polish.

Honing, filling and polish — the slow craft of cutting, matching and finishing stone, including the restoration of heritage surfaces back to life.

The Method

From block to surface

01

Quarry

Blocks are chosen at the source, the vein read in raw light before a single cut is committed.

02

Saw

Gang saws open the block into sequential slabs — each one a page of the same geological book.

03

Match

Consecutive slabs are opened like wings, book- and quad-matched into mirrored compositions.

04

Finish

Honed, polished, brushed or filled — the surface tuned to the hand as much as to the eye.
